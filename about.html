THE HOUSE / A POINT OF VIEW

Not everything rare is meant to be found.

THE VAULT is a curated digital auction destination for exceptional objects: fine watches, jewellery, art, classic automobiles and rare collectibles.

✳ An independent student demonstration project, shaped as an editorial study in collecting and competition.

01 / OUR PHILOSOPHY

Rarity deserves
a different kind of room.

01

Curation

Objects are presented with attention to their significance, character and the details that reward a second look.

02

Transparency

Auction information, estimates and available object details should be easy to find and clear to understand.

03

Competition

Bidding should feel focused, engaging and respectful of the object at the centre of the room.

02 / WHAT BELONGS IN THE VAULT
