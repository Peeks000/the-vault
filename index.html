HOME/ABOUT

ABOUT THE VAULT

Online auctions for rare collectibles.

THE VAULT is a digital auction platform built for discovering, inspecting, and bidding on watches, fine art, jewelry, and classic cars.

✳ Built as a first-year B.Tech Computer Science web project with HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Three.js, and Supabase.

01 / DESIGN GOALS

Built for clarity, speed, and trust.

01

Curated Lots

Only a focused edit of rare pieces appears at once, making it easy to compare items without noise.

02

Clear Pricing

Every item displays price estimates in INR, bid increments, countdowns, and full bid logs upfront.

03

Interactive Views

Inspect high-resolution galleries and a real-time 3D WebGL model before placing a bid.
